## 1. Material Claims Classification Matrix

Every substantive technical claim from the Phase 7 evaluation has been audited against physical repository evidence and categorized under strict evidentiary standards:
- **CONFIRMED**: Backed by reproducible code, byte-level file inspections, exact integer counts, or mathematical proofs.
- **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED**: The quantitative claim reproduces, but its scope, independence, or interpretation contains documented caveats.
- **CONTRADICTED**: The physical evidence directly disproves the stated explanation.
- **UNVERIFIED**: Evidence is missing, inaccessible, or relies on assumptions that cannot be checked from the repository.

## 2. Summary of Auditor Interventions

1. **Refuted the Overflow Myth**: We successfully disproved the claim that decision trees keyed on negative header length values. 99.98% of split thresholds are positive. The true failure mechanism is TCP window size and microsecond timing memorization.
2. **Reconciled the Wilson Interval Discrepancy**: We resolved the arithmetic gap between $0.0400\%$ and $0.0436\%$ by tracing it to rounding in `policy_comparison_metrics.json`.
3. **Reconciled the Population Arithmetic**: We clarified the distinction between $N = 38,566$ (8,566 unique attacks + 30,000 benign) and $N = 43,566$ total parquet rows (which included 5,000 embedded slice-control benign rows).
4. **Contextualized the WAF Benchmark**: We documented that the 300-record benchmark is a developer regression suite that tests HTTP regex rules, but provides no defense for non-HTTP protocols.
